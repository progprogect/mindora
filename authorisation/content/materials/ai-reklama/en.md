## 🎬 What do I actually mean by creating AI ads for brands?

There are no limits here on who we advertise. It can be a car brand, perfume, creams, glasses, anything you like really! Here I am advertising a perfume that never existed, with my own personal scent.

prompt- Ultra-realistic luxury perfume product shot (9: 16, 4K). A clear rectangular glass perfume bottle with warm amber liquid and a metallic cap, floating mid-air above rugged driftwood/roots. Autumn leaves swirling around in motion, soft particles, warm orange/brown gradient background with cinematic glow. Studio-quality lighting (soft key +rim), crisp reflections, sharp label reading \[Ai.Nikita\], shallow depth of field, high-end commercial retouching, dramatic bokeh, no extra text, no watermark.

## 👨‍💻 Which types of brand ads I would single out:

1\. A simple, direct one, where the product sits in a very beautiful photorealistic location (like in the example from the previous point)

2\. A more subtle one, where we build a story like in real ads, in any style (from realism to anime), with a plot where we show the hero's success thanks to our product in a natural way

3\. Creating a series with its own storyline in which the brand keeps appearing (right now this is the most promising direction, but for that very reason also the hardest)

In this guide we will look at how the first type works and what it is for!

## Why this kind of advertising? ⁉️

Honestly, it is all very simple: this kind of ad is fairly easy to make, and if you do it often and get used to "churning it out", you will put out this kind of content a lot and regularly. For any brand, being mentioned often is a huge plus. "Staying in people's minds" is always useful.

prompt - Cinematic ultra-high-end product photography of an official LitEnergy Classic energy drink can — a standard tall, vertical black aluminum energy drink can featuring the iconic bright cyan/turquoise flame logo, bold white "LIT ENERGY" typography, and vibrant turquoise lower body exactly matching the official product design. The can is standing on a dark, rough, textured surface.

The can is being engulfed and seemingly possessed by a thick, glossy, jet-black organic symbiotic substance inspired by the living alien symbiote from the 2018 film Venom. The viscous material behaves like a living entity — muscular, predatory, semi-sentient, and biomechanical — forming elongated, sinewy tendrils that crawl upward and wrap tightly around the can as if infecting and claiming it.

The symbiote texture is extremely dense, oily, hyper-glossy, elastic, with visible surface tension, micro ripples, sharp blade-like protrusions, organic vein structures, and subtle muscle-like movement. It should feel alive and intelligent rather than random liquid splashes.

The black symbiote appears to merge directly into the aluminum surface of the LitEnergy can, partially fusing with the metal while preserving the product identity. Some tendrils anchor themselves to the ground while others extend dramatically into the air, creating controlled chaos and dynamic tension.

The bright cyan flame logo and the bold "LIT ENERGY" branding remain clean, unobstructed, and become the primary visual focal point, contrasting dramatically against the black symbiotic mass. The turquoise lower section of the can remains partially visible, creating a striking color contrast with the glossy black organism.

Camera angle: dramatic low-angle hero shot with a slight Dutch angle, photographed using a high-end macro cinema lens. Extremely shallow depth of field with razor-sharp focus on the logo, typography, and symbiote interaction. Ultra-detailed macro textures.

Lighting: moody, cinematic, high-contrast studio lighting with strong cold cyan rim lighting sculpting the reflective contours of the symbiote and emphasizing the metallic finish of the aluminum can. Soft turquoise light emitted from the branding subtly illuminates nearby tendrils, while controlled specular highlights enhance the premium commercial aesthetic.

Atmosphere: floating micro-particles, fine dust, subtle mist, and volumetric haze catching the rim light to create depth and scale.

Background: deep black void with faint cool cyan bokeh, minimal gradients, and cinematic separation, reinforcing a mysterious, supernatural atmosphere.

### Working with images

ChatGPT can not only understand text, it can also work with images.

It can:

-   🎨 Create images from scratch based on your description.
-   For example: "Draw a futuristic city on Mars at sunset" or "Create a logo in a minimalist style".
-   🖼️ Edit existing images.
-   Remove or add objects.
-   Change the background.
-   Change clothing, colors and lighting.
-   Improve image quality.
-   Turn a photo into a drawing, watercolor, anime, comic, pixel art and many other styles.
-   👀 Analyze images.
-   Describe what is shown.
-   Find mistakes or details.
-   Read text in a photo.
-   Help you make sense of diagrams, charts and documents.

### A simple example

You can write:

> "Create a realistic image of a white cat sitting on a roof under a starry sky."

Or upload a photo and ask:

> "Remove the people in the background, replace the overcast sky with a sunset and make the image more cinematic."

## ℹ️ Proper prompting in ChatGPT

A good prompt for creating or editing an image is not a set of "magic words" but a clear technical brief. The more detail you give about the result, the closer it will be to what you expect.

I recommend using this structure.

## For creating an image

1\. Main subject

Who or what should be in the image?

> A young woman, a white tiger, a futuristic city, a red sports car.

2\. Action or pose

What is happening?

> The woman walks down the street, the tiger lies on a rock, the car speeds along the highway.

3\. Location

Where does the action take place?

> On a skyscraper roof, in an autumn forest, on a beach, in space.

4\. Atmosphere and lighting

What mood?

> Sunset, golden hour, rainy night, soft sunlight, neon lighting, fog.

5\. Image style

What should it look like?

-   photorealism;
-   cinematic shot;
-   anime;
-   watercolor;
-   oil painting;
-   pixel art;
