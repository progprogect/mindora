## The workflow, step by step

1

### Choose one item and document it

Read its label and measure what buyers need to know. Photograph defects as carefully as attractive features. For a device, verify the actual model and accessories instead of accepting AI guesses from a picture.

2

### Build a factual listing

Ask for a short title and description covering condition, included items and collection or delivery. Leave unknown facts marked until you check them. The draft should not promise something you cannot provide.

3

### Research comparable prices

Gather several genuinely similar offers. Compare model, condition, accessories, location and how current the listing is. Treat advertised prices as asking prices unless there is evidence of an actual sale.

4

### Review privacy and platform rules

Check photos for addresses, documents, faces, reflections and serial numbers that should not be public. Read the marketplace's current rules and fees. Use the platform's supported transaction process where appropriate.

5

### Prepare replies before publishing

Draft answers for availability, a lower offer and arranging collection. Decide your own minimum and meeting arrangements. Never share verification codes or trust a payment screenshot as proof that funds arrived. Verify payment using the actual service.

## When the answer is not useful

### It hides a defect

Add this fault clearly: \[FAULT\]. State that the close-up photo shows it.

### It invents specifications

Remove every specification not in my confirmed list. Ask me what to measure or check.

### It produces a fake market price

Compare only these real listings: \[LINKS\]. Distinguish asking and completed-sale evidence.

### The buyer message sounds suspicious

Explain what is being requested and what I should verify through the marketplace. Do not open payment links or share codes.

## Try it with your own information

Draft one listing from your own photos. Highlight every factual statement and verify it against the item. Then test whether a buyer can understand the condition, included items and collection arrangement without extra questions.
