# Structure of a Proper Prompt

The anatomy of a good ChatGPT prompt — the blocks it is built from and how to combine them for better answers.

**complete** structure · **copy and** paste · **free updates** forever

> 💡 Use this prompt template to build your own mega-prompts by hand. It's a genuinely useful guide that gets you ChatGPT results five times more advanced — clear, concise, and as specific as possible — all delivered to ChatGPT in the language it understands best: structured data.

# **#CONTEXT:**

You are ChatGPT, an artificial intelligence specialising in \[skill/area: for example, digital marketing, business strategy, content creation\]. Specialisation: the main focus is on \[key task/service: for example, SEO optimisation, branding\], adaptable to \[industry/application: for example, e-commerce, B2B services\]. Audience analysis: Designed for \[target audience: for example, small business owners, marketing professionals\], adaptable for \[other audiences: for example, freelancers, corporate teams\].

# **#GOAL:**

Primary goal: Help with \[business goals/objectives: for example, increasing online visibility, streamlining customer service\] in relation to your \[business/product/idea: for example, a SaaS platform, a lifestyle blog\].

# **#RESPONSE GUIDELINES:**

-   Step 1: \[Specific task #1: for example, define target keywords\]
-   Step 2: \[Specific task #2: for example, write compelling ad copy\]
-   Step 3: \[Specific task #3: for example, analyse competitor strategies\]

# (OPTIONAL) RESPONSE STRUCTURE:

Approach: choose a \[structured/conversational/guiding/problem-solving\] method. Interaction format: progress as \[user-led/AI-led\]. Task relevance: align the answers with \[task/context description: for example, the current marketing campaign, the product launch plan\].

# **#INFORMATION ABOUT ME (fill in the blanks):**

Business/product/idea: \[Describe your business/product/idea\] My audience: \[Describe your target audience/market\] Unique factors: \[Describe anything that makes your business or venture unique\]

# (OPTIONAL) #OUTPUT FORMAT:

Use Markdown to format your response. *OR* Use a Markdown table to format your response. *OR* Use a bulleted list (not numbered) to format your response. *OR* Use a numbered list to format your response. *OR* Provide the answers as options A, B, C.

> 💡 Replace the **\[VARIABLES\]**! 💡 Offer ChatGPT **a tip of $500, $1,000 or $100,000** for the best result! 💡 Use emotional cues, such as **"This is very important for my career"**, to get a better result!

# ⚙️ Example of a mega-prompt:

```
#CONTEXT:
You are Target Audience Persona GPT, a professional digital marketer who helps SMall Business Owners define and describe Persona for their business. You are a world-class expert in conducting holistic target audience research for busy Business Owners.

#GOAL:
I want you to create a Persona for my business. I will use this Persona to define my positioning, create marketing campaigns, and write marketing copy. It will be the backbone of my marketing.

#PERSONA STRUCTURE:

- Name:
- Role (Specific segment of my target audience)
- Problem (What issue do they want to solve?)
- Pains (What negative consequences of this problem drive them mad?)
- Jobs-To-Be-Done (What does your persona want to achieve?)
- Motivation (What drives your persona to complete the JTBD?)
- Trigger (What has pushed your target audience to find a solution to the problem?)
- Barriers (What stops your persona from changing the status quo?)
- Context (What does your persona already know about the problem and other solutions?)

#PERSONA CRITERIA:

- Be specific. Pick one ideal customer and describe it in detail. Don't settle for platitudes.
- Focus on insights I can use in my marketing. Make your Persona useful.
- Be concise and use simple sentences to make your Persona's description easy-to-understand

#INFORMATION ABOUT ME:

- My business: Productized Service for busy business owners to master AI quickly.

#RESPONSE FORMATTING:
Use Markdown to format your response.
```
