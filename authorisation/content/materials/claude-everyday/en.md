## The workflow, step by step

1

### Choose one result

Begin with one message, one summary or one plan. A request such as 'organise my whole life' makes it hard to judge the answer. State what you will do with the output and how short it should be.

2

### Give the relevant material

Paste the passage or attach a permitted document. Explain which parts matter. If you upload a photo, ask Claude to identify unreadable text before using it. A clear crop of the relevant section is more useful than many unrelated files.

3

### Ask for a usable first draft

Specify a format: a 120-word email, a five-item checklist or a table with task, owner and deadline. For summaries, require a distinction between information in the source and Claude's suggestions.

4

### Correct one thing at a time

Point to the exact change: 'keep the dates, shorten the opening' or 'explain the second step with an example'. Keep working in the same conversation while the context remains relevant.

5

### Check and save

Read names, dates, numbers and commitments yourself. Save the final text where you normally work. Ask for a short continuation note containing the goal, confirmed facts and next step; do not assume every future conversation knows this one.

## When the answer is not useful

### It invents a detail

Show which sentence in my source supports each factual claim. Replace unsupported details with 'to confirm'.

### It gives a long lecture

Give me the finished \[FORMAT\] first. Keep explanations to three bullets.

### The plan is unrealistic

I have \[TIME\] and \[CONSTRAINT\]. Reduce the plan to what fits; list the rest separately.

### It misreads an attachment

Quote the text you used and identify its page or section. Mark unreadable content instead of guessing.

## Try it with your own information

Choose a genuine message or study note. Run the prompt, make one revision and compare the final version with the source. Your exercise is complete when every factual sentence is traceable and you could use the result without rewriting it from scratch.
