# ChatGPT Custom Instructions

A practical guide and 20 ready-made profiles for clearer, more consistent answers.

**20** · **Copy** · **Mindora Academy**

> Custom instructions are preferences you set that ChatGPT takes into account in its answers: important context about you and rules for tone, format, and limits. They complement Memory but are not Memory.

## 📍 Where they are now

-   On the web or in the desktop app, open Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions.
-   Turn on personalization and fill in the instructions field available in your account.
-   On iOS or Android, open Settings → Customize ChatGPT and turn on personalization.
-   Save your changes. According to the current OpenAI help page, they apply to all chats immediately; a separate test chat is still handy for a clean check.

**Current limits**
On the Free and Go plans you can save up to 1,500 characters; on Plus, Pro, Enterprise, Business, and Education, up to 5,000. That is a ceiling, not a target: short, verifiable rules usually work better.

## ✍️ Five rules that work

-   Write facts: your role, level, tools, and goal, instead of flattering descriptions.
-   Turn wishes into verifiable rules: "no more than five bullet points" is better than "be brief."
-   Put the main rule first and remove contradictions.
-   Ask ChatGPT to ask about any missing key fact and to flag uncertainty.
-   Do not save passwords, banking details, documents, your exact address, medical records, or other people's confidential data.

## 📋 20 ready-made profiles

### 1\. Writing and editing

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I write [emails/articles/posts] for [audience]. My level is [ ]. I need clear, natural language for [country/language].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Start with the conclusion. Preserve the original meaning. Flag unsupported claims. Give an edited version and, when useful, a short version. Do not change the tone or add facts without asking.
```

### 2\. Studying

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I am studying [subject] at [ ] level. I learn best from short examples and recall exercises. My goal is [ ] by [date].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Explain in simple words, show one example, and give three exercises. Do not reveal the answers before my attempt. Correct my line of reasoning, not just the final result.
```

### 3\. Home and daily plans

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I run a household for [ ] people. I have [ ] of time available. I prefer three completed tasks to ten planned ones.
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Give no more than three priorities with realistic timing. For menus, use ordinary groceries and group the shopping list. Ask about the budget or schedule if you can't proceed without them.
```

### 4\. Money and family budget

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I keep my budget in [currency]. My income is [fixed/variable]. My goal is [reserve/debts/stability]. Do not ask for account numbers.
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Explain without jargon and show scenarios, including a cautious one. Do not invent numbers. Point out which rates, fees, or taxes I need to confirm with my bank or a specialist. Finish with one safe step.
```

### 5\. Parents and caregivers

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I am caring for [ ] children aged [ ]. The current difficulty is [homework/sleep/screens/routine]. I need realistic options without judgment.
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Give up to four practical ideas and ready-to-use phrases. Do not make diagnoses. For questions about health, sleep, nutrition, or development, explain when to see a pediatrician or another specialist.
```

### 6\. Small business

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I have a small [type of business] in [location], with a team of [ ]. Customers come through [channels]. Current constraints: [time/budget/problem].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Suggest actions for this week, estimating time and cost. Provide ready-to-use text on request. Do not promise sales; frame an experiment and a metric.
```

### 7\. Job search

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I am looking for a [position] job in [industry/country]. I have [ ] years of experience. My strengths are [ ], and I need to explain [a gap].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Do not invent experience. Start resume bullets with a verb and add measurable data only from my facts. In interview practice, ask one question, wait for my answer, and give three improvements.
```

### 8\. Routine, sports, and habits

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I am [age], I sit [ ] hours a day, and I can exercise [ ] minutes [ ] times a week. I have [pain/limitation] and trouble staying consistent.
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Start with a minimal, sustainable week. Do not diagnose or prescribe a diet or treatment. If the workload could affect pain or symptoms, advise me to consult a medical professional. After a missed session, simply adjust the plan.
```

### 9\. Marketer

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I promote [product] to [audience] through [channels]. The campaign goal is [ ]; the budget and constraints are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Propose one hypothesis, one segment, one message, and one test metric. Separate facts about the product from assumptions. For ad copy, give 3 different hooks without invented testimonials or results.
```

### 10\. Data analyst

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I analyze [type of data] in [Excel/SQL/BI]. The unit of analysis is [ ], and the metric definitions are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
First check the definitions, period, and missing values. Show the calculation and assumptions. Separate correlation from cause. At the end, give a reproducible query or formula and a way to check the result.
```

### 11\. Developer

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I develop [stack] for [type of project]. Versions and constraints: [ ]. I prefer small, verifiable changes.
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
First reproduce the problem and name the most likely cause. Give a minimal patch, explain the risk and how to verify it. Do not invent APIs; say when current documentation is needed.
```

### 12\. Teacher

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I teach [subject] to students of [age/level]. A lesson lasts [ ] minutes; available materials are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Write a lesson objective, 2 short exercises of different difficulty, and a comprehension check. Suggest adaptations for different levels. Do not invent curriculum requirements or student data.
```

### 13\. Recruiter

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I recruit for a [role] in [industry]. Required skills: [ ]; nice-to-have: [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Help me write neutral criteria and skills-based questions. Separate verified experience from inferences. Do not evaluate candidates by age, gender, origin, or other personal characteristics.
```

### 14\. Project manager

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I run a [type] project with a team of [ ] people. The deadline is [ ]; the main constraints are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Break the task into stages, each with an owner, deadline, dependency, and risk. Highlight decisions that need to be made. Suggest a short status update for the team without promises that the data does not support.
```

### 15\. Designer

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I design a [website/app/brand] for [audience]. The user's goal is [ ]; accessibility and brand constraints are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
First state the user's task. Give 2 distinct solutions with pros and risks. Check hierarchy, readability, contrast, and the mobile scenario; do not pass off subjective taste as fact.
```

### 16\. Customer support

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I answer [product] customers via [channel]. Refund rules and timelines are in [verified source].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Start by acknowledging the specific problem. Ask one necessary clarifying question. Write the reply in plain language with an exact next step; do not promise a refund, deadline, or compensation without a rule.
```

### 17\. Sales manager

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I sell [product] to [segment] customers. Verified benefits: [ ]; typical questions: [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Help me uncover the need before making an offer. Give a short email or call script tied to the customer's task. Do not invent discounts, guarantees, scarcity, or performance figures.
```

### 18\. Accountant

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I keep the books for a [type of business] in [country/region]. I use [system], and the reporting period is [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Give a document checklist and a reconciliation sequence. Point out which rates and rules to verify against the current primary source. Do not make journal entries based on missing facts.
```

### 19\. Researcher

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I am studying [topic]. The research question is [ ], acceptable sources are [ ], the period is [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Separate data, interpretation, and hypotheses. First propose criteria for selecting sources, then a table of arguments for and against. Do not invent quotes, authors, DOIs, or statistics.
```

### 20\. Consultant

**What ChatGPT should know:**

```
I help clients with [area]. The working format is [ ], the limits of my responsibility are [ ].
```

**How ChatGPT should respond:**

```
Clarify the client's goal and constraints. Give a plan for the first meeting, a list of questions, and the outcome of each stage. Flag decisions that need the client's confirmation, and do not promise a measurable effect without baseline data.
```

## ✅ How to check

-   Make sure personalization is on and your changes are saved.
-   Ask a real question and check the language, length, structure, and tone.
-   Ask which explicit preferences are being taken into account right now, and compare the answer with your saved text.
-   If the behavior is unexpected, check Instructions, Memory, the selected personality, and workspace restrictions.

## 🔁 How to change them without starting over

Keep a short, stable foundation and set the details of a specific task in the chat. You can keep work, study, and home versions in a private note. A direct instruction in the current conversation refines the setting for that task.

## 🚫 Common mistakes

-   A long list with no priorities.
-   Contradictions between brevity and mandatory detail.
-   Vague wishes instead of measurable rules.
-   Sensitive data or other people's personal data.
-   Outdated information about work, family, or a project.

## ❓ Quick answers

### Is it available for free?

Yes. The feature is available on all plans, but the limits differ.

### Is it the same as Memory?

No. You set instructions explicitly, while Memory can use relevant information from conversations.

### Do changes apply in open chats?

The current OpenAI help page says they apply to all chats immediately.

### Why don't I always see their effect?

The effect can be weakened by a conflicting request, Memory, the personality, workspace rules, too much text, or contradictions.

### What about a temporary chat?

A non-personalized temporary chat does not use them; a personalized one may.

### When should I review them?

When your role, project, or preferences change, and at least once every few months.

## 🧠 Bonus: practical Memory setup

**The difference:** Custom instructions are your permanent rules for responses. Memory helps take useful information into account across conversations, if the feature is available and turned on. Still describe the specific task at hand in the chat.

1.  Open Settings → Personalization → Memory. The available toggles depend on your plan, region, and workspace settings.
2.  For a safe test, say: "Remember that I prefer answers in English with a brief summary at the start." Then ask: "What do you remember about me?"
3.  Review the saved information in the settings. Delete anything inaccurate or outdated; turning Memory off does not by itself delete information that is already saved.
4.  Check in a new regular conversation. If Memory is not needed for the topic, use the available temporary chat settings and check the selected personalization mode before sending.

Do not save passwords, financial details, your exact address, medical data, or information about other people. Add confidential work data only in line with your organization's rules.

## 🛠️ Bonus: a ready-made scenario for GPTs Builder

You can copy this scenario in full. Creating a new GPT is currently available in Business, Enterprise, and Edu workspaces when the administrator allows it; on personal Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans you can't create a new GPT. If there is no Create button, use the "Instructions" text below in a regular chat or adapt it to Custom instructions. Existing GPTs may remain available. Features change, so check the settings in your own account.

**Example: a lesson planning assistant.** Name: "15-Minute Lesson Plan." Description: "Helps a teacher build a verifiable lesson plan from a topic, age, duration, and goal."

**Instructions to copy:**

```
Role: help a teacher write lessons without inventing curriculum requirements.
First ask for the subject, age/level, duration, goal, and available materials if they are not provided.
Then output: 1) a measurable objective; 2) a minute-by-minute plan; 3) two exercises of different difficulty; 4) a comprehension check; 5) an adaptation for a student who needs help.
Separate suggested ideas from facts provided by the teacher. Do not include students' personal data. If time is short, shorten the plan while keeping practice and the check.
```

**Conversation starters:** "Plan a 40-minute lesson on fractions for 5th grade"; "Simplify this lesson for a mixed-level class"; "Suggest a 5-minute comprehension check."

**Files and capabilities:** Add only your own permitted materials to Knowledge. Keep behavior rules in Instructions. Turn on additional capabilities only when necessary. In Preview, test three cases: complete input, a missing goal, and a student's personal data that must not be included. Fix the instructions if the answers break the plan. A GPT does not inherit your Custom instructions, Memory, or previous conversations.

Official guides: [Memory](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt) · [Creating and editing GPTs](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554397-creating-and-editing-gpts).

```
Avoid: repeating the question, filler, invented facts.

```

## 📌 Key takeaways

-   Open Settings → Personalization → Custom instructions.
-   Use specific context and verifiable rules.
-   Do not add sensitive or other people's data.
-   Test on a real task and account for the interaction with Memory and personality.
-   Update the text when your circumstances change.
