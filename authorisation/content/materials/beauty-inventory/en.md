## Your practical result

A simple product inventory, a list of duplicate functions and a deliberate purchase check. You will know which details are confirmed and which labels or dates still need checking.

A hands-on guide with a reusable prompt, a worked example and a short practice task. Use it at your own pace.

## A simple route through the task

What is on your shelf → Check purpose + stock → Buy, replace or wait

## The workflow, step by step

1

### Inventory one small category

Start with serums, cleansers or makeup rather than photographing the whole room. Give every product a simple ID so similar packaging cannot be confused.

2

### Capture useful label details

Photograph name, directions and relevant expiry information. Write dates only when you know them. A package symbol and a manufacturing code are not interchangeable; ask the manufacturer when information is unclear.

3

### Group by purpose

Ask for a table showing which products serve similar functions. The assistant can flag overlap without declaring different formulations identical or clinically interchangeable.

4

### Create a use-and-review list

Choose products to review based on labels, known opening dates and current condition. Do not make finishing a product more important than its instructions or safety. If smell, colour, texture or packaging changes, stop and check rather than asking AI to certify it.

5

### Run every purchase through the table

Ask what gap the new product fills, which existing item overlaps and whether there is a real replacement need. Save the decision and update stock after a purchase so the inventory stays useful.

## When the answer is not useful

### It guesses an expiry date

Replace inferred dates with 'unknown'. Show the package information needed to determine it.

### It treats all serums as identical

Compare their stated purposes and label facts; mark formulation differences that are not confirmed.

### It pressures me to finish an old product

Prioritise label instructions and condition checks. Do not recommend continued use just to avoid waste.

### The inventory is too much work

Reduce the table to product, category, opened date, label information and quantity left.

## Try it with your own information

Record five products from one shelf and check the table against the bottles. Find one overlap and one genuinely missing piece of information. Run a real shopping temptation through the comparison before deciding.
