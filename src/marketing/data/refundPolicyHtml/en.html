Refund Policy

What can and cannot be refunded. Please read before you buy.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

The short version

  • Access to digital content starts immediately after payment, so paid purchases are not refundable if you change your mind, don't use the materials, or have already downloaded them.
  • This applies to Entry Products, paid trials, subscriptions, upgrades and add-ons.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time before the next charge: there will be no further charges and you keep access until the end of the paid period. The period already paid for is not refunded.
  • A refund within 14 calendar days is available only for a course purchased separately from a subscription, as a separate order. Courses available inside a subscription are not covered: the subscription gives access to all materials on the platform and is not refundable.

This Refund Policy applies to purchases on Mindora Academy (mindoraacademy.com), operated by Scalion Ltd, and forms part of our Terms of Use. Earlier purchases remain subject to the terms in effect at the time of purchase.

1. What you buy

Mindora Academy is a digital learning platform. Depending on the offer, you may purchase:

  • Entry Products — one-time digital products offered at the start of an offer (for example, AI Starter Kit, Claude AI Guide Kit, 28-Day AI Plan). Charged once, not renewed, with lifetime access. They do not include a subscription.
  • Paid trial subscription — a subscription that starts with a paid trial for a small fee (for example, US$1 for 7 days). Unless cancelled before the trial ends, it automatically converts to a paid subscription at the regular price shown at purchase (for example, US$29 per month).
  • Membership (subscription) — recurring access to the full library. It starts only after you separately confirm it on the offer page and renews automatically until cancelled.
  • Upgrades and plan changes — moving to a different or higher subscription plan.
  • Add-ons — one-time products offered after the main purchase (for example, AI Coach "Ask Mindora", AI Prompt Library, Planners). Lifetime access that remains if your subscription ends.
  • Standalone courses — individual courses and certification programs purchased as a separate order, outside a subscription. A course you take as part of your subscription is not a separate purchase.

The offer page and checkout screen always show exactly what you are buying: a one-time product or a subscription trial. Access starts as soon as payment is confirmed, and downloadable materials can be saved right away. The price does not depend on how much content you use or how quickly.

2. Price, trial and renewal

Your order is defined by the product, amount, currency, billing period, trial length and renewal price shown on the offer page and checkout screen before you confirm the purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period until cancelled. We notify existing subscribers in advance of any change to the regular price so they can cancel. A period already paid for is not recalculated.

3. Cancelling your subscription

  • You can cancel at any time in the subscription section of your account or by emailing support@mindoraacademy.com. To avoid the next charge, cancel before the next billing date shown in your account.
  • If you cancel during a free or paid trial, you will not be charged the regular subscription price.
  • After cancellation you keep access until the end of the trial or billing period already started, and no further charges are made.
  • Cancelling is not a refund request. It only stops future charges.
  • Cancelling does not affect Entry Products, add-ons or standalone courses you have purchased.

4. Refunds

Access to digital content starts immediately after payment. Therefore, once access has been provided, purchases are not refundable because of a change of mind or plans, non-use of the materials, or their quick use or download — to the extent permitted by applicable law.

5. Consumer rights and when we always refund

Consumers in the United Kingdom and the European Union generally have a 14-day right to cancel distance contracts. For digital content, this right is lost once supply has begun if, at checkout, you expressly agreed to immediate access and acknowledged that you lose your right to cancel. If mandatory law in your country nevertheless gives you a right to cancel or to a prorated refund, we will honour it.

We will always refund or otherwise resolve:

  • duplicate or erroneous charges;
  • charges you did not authorise;
  • a paid product you could not access because of a confirmed technical error on our side.

6. Earlier purchases

Purchases made before the date of this policy remain subject to the refund terms in effect at the time of purchase. This update does not worsen the terms of earlier purchases.

7. How to contact us

Email support@mindoraacademy.com from the address you used at purchase. Please include:

  • the product or plan;

  • your Stripe receipt number (if available);
  • a short description of your request.

We reply within five business days; mandatory legal deadlines take priority. Approved refunds are issued through Stripe to the original payment method; funds usually arrive within 5–10 business days.
